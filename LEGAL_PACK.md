## 2. Intellectual property (IP) ownership statement

### 2.1 Ownership declaration
All intellectual property rights in TRACE-AI — including source code, software
architecture, documentation, datasets lawfully created by the Authors and owned
by them, technical reports,
and associated intellectual property — are initially and
exclusively owned by **Sadia Rehman & Muhammad Amin ur Rehman Khan**, unless
explicitly transferred under written agreement.

> This work is created independently by the Authors and is not subject to any
> employment, consultancy, or work-for-hire arrangement with IASRD unless
> explicitly stated in a separate written agreement.

> Nothing in this document shall be construed as creating a partnership,
> employment relationship, or implied transfer of intellectual property rights.

### 2.2 IASRD role
IASRD acts as development facilitator, institutional host, and collaborator.

> IASRD is acknowledged as an institutional collaborator for branding and
> research-affiliation purposes only and does not claim ownership rights unless
> explicitly assigned in writing.

### 2.3 Assignment clause
IP may only be transferred via a written IP Assignment Deed, signed by all
original authors, with board approval if IASRD is to become a co-owner.

## 3. Open source & licensing framework

The Work is licensed under **Apache License 2.0** as the sole and active licence.
Any future licence change shall require a written versioned release and explicit
authors' approval.

Any change in licensing shall constitute a new version release and shall not
affect prior versions.

**Future open-core model (intended, not yet built):**
- *Open:* core TRACE-AI engine, research modules.
- *Closed / premium:* dashboards, institutional analytics, API access layer,
  workflow automation tools.

Note: a premium closed product should be built as a **separate codebase /
feature set** so the open licence terms are not in question, and — given the
charity — likely delivered through an appropriate trading vehicle after advice.

## 4. Contributor agreement (open-source)

### 4.1 Purpose
External contributors agree that contributions become part of the TRACE-AI
ecosystem and that contributions are licensed under the project terms or under a
Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA), depending on the contributor agreement in
place.

### 4.2 Clause
> Contributors grant a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to their
> contributions, unless a separate Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA) assigning
> IP is signed.

### 4.3 Moral rights
Contributors retain attribution rights to their contributions. Nothing in this
framework entitles contributors to ownership of the TRACE-AI project as a whole
unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 6. IASRD internal governance policy (software assets)

### 6.1 Authority structure
- *Founders / authors:* ultimate authority regarding intellectual property
  decisions (unless transferred).
- *IASRD Board:* oversight only.
- *Technical lead:* implementation authority.

### 6.2 Approval requirements
Formal approval is required for: commercialisation, licensing change, external
partnership, API monetisation, or spin-off creation.

### 6.3 Charity compliance
> Any commercial or trading activity arising from TRACE-AI shall be conducted in
> compliance with UK charity law and may be carried out through a separate
> trading subsidiary if required.

### 6.4 Data protection
> Any processing of personal data shall be carried out in accordance with UK GDPR
> and the Data Protection Act 2018.

### 6.5 Asset classification
- Core Research Asset — the TRACE-AI engine.
- Institutional Asset — deployments.
- Commercial Asset — future products.
